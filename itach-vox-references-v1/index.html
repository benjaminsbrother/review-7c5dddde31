The motion rules we will follow

  1. Hard cuts only. Nothing dissolves.
  2. A graphic may run 10 seconds or more, if something inside keeps changing.
  3. Push-ins on flat scans are small and slow: 2 to 5 percent over 3 to 7 seconds.
  4. A snap push to a detail is fast and eased out: 12 to 15 frames.
  5. Highlight bar wipes in 0.3 to 0.5 seconds, then holds 3 to 4 seconds. Yellow is the common colour.
  6. Label chips pop in over about 3 frames, no bounce, and hold 2.5 to 3.5 seconds.
  7. Arrows draw along their path, tail to head, about 1 second each, head last.
  8. Counters and typed text ease. Typing runs about 11 characters per second.
  9. No burned word captions. Labels, headlines and quoted documents only.
  10. Every graphic sits on a texture: paper, desk, grain. Never a bare colour.
  11. Stacked photo panels pop in staggered, about 0.15 seconds apart. Not sliding.
  12. One accent colour per piece, matched to the subject.
